How an experiment gets here

  1. 01Read

    New arXiv and graphics papers stream into a queue. Most are skipped within seconds.

  2. 02Judge

    A brief asks what is genuinely new, what it enables, and whether anyone has already built it.

  3. 03Build

    Agents do not just re-implement the paper. They fuse its idea with something else, another technique, a game mechanic, a new kind of interface, and push until the result could not have existed a month ago.

  4. 04Show

    The finished repo is pinned to an exact commit and appears here, sandboxed, with the paper credited.
